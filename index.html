Introduction

Small targets, subtle attributes and changing viewpoints make UAV imagery challenging for vision–language models. Reliable answers require models to discover task-relevant visual clues, interpret their semantic and spatial meaning, and connect multiple observations to support a conclusion.

Drone-PUR is a dataset and benchmark organized around these three capability dimensions. Its 11 tasks span single images, image sequences, aligned RGB–thermal observations and synchronized UAV views. Training resources and complementary multiple-choice and open-ended evaluations support both capability assessment and domain adaptation.

Drone-PUR Benchmark

Drone-PUR brings together 168K images and 620K bounding-box annotations. Around 90.2% of task-relevant boxes occupy less than 1% of an image, reflecting the fine-grained demands of UAV observation. Training and test splits are disjoint at the image and video levels.

All test samples are human-reviewed. The Position Spatial Judgment task is entirely human-annotated and covers vehicle heading, pedestrian heading, turning direction, object-relative position and vertical-level comparison. Evaluation combines ten multiple-choice tasks with direct detection, grounding and counting.

UAV-PUR: a unified adaptation baseline.

Built on Qwen3-VL at the 4B and 8B scales, UAV-PUR combines stage-wise fine-tuning with geometric and numerical auxiliary supervision. All tasks retain the original language-generation interface at inference.
